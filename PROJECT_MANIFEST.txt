Architecture:
- 512 m Terrain Streaming Cells independent from simulation chunks and render LOD
- WorkerThreadPool data-only generation
- one running job + latest pending request
- predictive prefetch from tangential velocity
- double-buffered old/new surface
- staged main-thread ArrayMesh/collision/MultiMesh commit
- revision-based cancellation
