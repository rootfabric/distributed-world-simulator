Architecture test additions:
- independent real-scale Earth body: radius 6,371,000 m
- Moon remains at radius 1,737,400 m and keeps the original v15.2 implementation
- real simulation-space Earth-Moon center distance: 384,400,000 m
- floating-origin Earth view and angular-size Earth proxy from the Moon
- validated procedural rule pipeline: relief -> hydrology -> climate -> biome -> surface
- explicit requires/writes ownership prevents hidden rule conflicts
- forest, grassland, rocky desert, tundra, alpine snow, ocean, rivers and lakes
- four procedural tree forms, three grass types and three rock variants
- deterministic nonuniform placement with water/snow/slope/rock filters
- MultiMesh vegetation and tiered geometry/billboard/removal LOD
- Earth/Moon startup policy is selected by the world catalog without changing lunar terrain code
- dedicated Earth runtime filters the celestial body catalog to Earth only
- common display/input/world commands stay owned by SimulatorApp in every world
