Real Scale Procedural Moon v15.3 — First-person World Interaction

Core changes:
- central first-person physics ray with a 6 m interaction distance
- reusable world_interactable contract
- focus descriptor, HUD card and material-overlay outline
- E toggles the Survey Beacon signal
- beacon_state and landmark.enabled are persisted per chunk
- component changes are appended to the world journal
- inactive beacon markers and terrain cache pins are disabled
- distant landmark font reduced from 56 to 11 and outline from 12 to 2
- existing v15.2 recent/pinned surface cache remains intact

Streaming and cache retained from v15.2:
- in-memory LRU cache of ready LOCAL terrain resources
- 8 recent cells plus up to 8 additional pinned beacon cells
- early pinned-cell return trigger at 1800 m with approach-direction guard
- cache hit can preempt a stale background generation job
- cached ArrayMesh, collision Shape3D and rock MultiMesh resources are reused
- sparse persistent landmark index at user://worlds/<world_id>/landmarks.json
