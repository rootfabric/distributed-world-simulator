## Goal

Prove that world/canonical complexity may grow to large structures while expensive physical detail remains sparse and causal:

```text
large canonical structure
        ↓
mostly BAKED / reduced
        ↓
local overload / impact
        ↓
certified guard
        ↓
bounded LOCAL FULL island
        ↓
canonical fracture/topology mutation
        ↓
functional consequences
        ↓
settle
        ↓
local rebake
```

Canonical Construction/Matter remains the truth. FABRIC owns only derived physical execution representations.

## Mandatory acceptance

Each scale must preserve:

1. one canonical source truth and no derived canonical writes;
2. one active physical owner per physical region;
3. stale bake never executes after canonical mutation;
4. local guard reveals only the certified causal island;
5. continuity across BAKE → LOCAL FULL within declared tolerance;
6. canonical topology/damage mutation is external to the representation controller;
7. settled fragments can rebake and execute again;
8. no lost or duplicated lifecycle event;
9. crash/restart cannot resurrect stale ownership or duplicate a committed mutation;
10. deterministic fresh-process replay of state/transition/work hashes.
